glyph-matter

Live font sampling, or a shipped pack. Font size is the on-screen size of the letters. Legibility is how hard the points try to remain a word.

animation

Move the pointer over the letters. Hold to push harder.

The first word is “text” above. Add more words to the loop. The in-between is how ink travels from one word to the next.

Two words sit apart, spring into a meeting point, then become the third. Force below is the collision (vortex if you have none).

effects

How hard ink tries to remain a word. 1 = locked letters; 0 = gas.

Random jitter when letters loosen. Full during dissolve (low legibility); none while a word is locked.

Wind is a traveling gust: the positive half of a sawtooth. Period is the cycle (slider × 0.1 s). Wavelength is how far a gust spans along the wind; 0 = the whole field pulses together.

0,0 is the word center. Click the canvas to place the well. Radius is how far it reaches.
